Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026
PDF Organizer is designed to organize PDF documents stored on your device. PDF file contents are processed locally and are not uploaded to Divium Labs servers.

1. About PDF Organizer

PDF Organizer is an Android application developed by Divium Labs that helps users find, organize, view, rename, share, favorite, categorize, and manage PDF files stored on their device.

2. File and Storage Access

PDF Organizer may request broad storage access in order to locate PDF files stored across the user's device. This access is essential to the application's core document-management functionality.

Storage access may be used to:

  • Scan the device for PDF files.
  • Display and organize PDF files.
  • Open PDF files using compatible applications.
  • Rename PDF files when requested by the user.
  • Share PDF files when requested by the user.
  • Delete or move PDF files to the system trash when requested by the user.
  • Create and manage local favorites and collections.

Divium Labs does not remotely access, upload, read, analyze, sell, or store the contents of your PDF documents on its own servers.

3. Local Data

Information such as the indexed PDF library, favorites, collections, preferences, and application settings may be stored locally on the user's device in order to provide the application's functionality.

This locally stored information may be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application.

4. Purchases and Premium Features

PDF Organizer may offer optional Premium purchases or subscriptions.

Purchases are processed through Google Play. PDF Organizer may also use RevenueCat to manage purchase status, subscriptions, entitlements, and restoration of purchases.

Divium Labs does not directly receive or store users' full payment card information. Payment processing is handled by Google Play and its associated services.

5. Third-Party Services

PDF Organizer may use third-party services necessary for application functionality, including:

  • Google Play services and Google Play Billing.
  • RevenueCat for purchase and entitlement management.

These providers may process limited technical or transaction-related information according to their own privacy practices.

6. Data Sharing

Divium Labs does not sell users' personal information or PDF contents.

Data may only be processed by service providers when necessary to provide features such as purchases and subscription management, or when required by applicable law.

7. Data Security

PDF Organizer is designed to minimize external data processing. PDF documents are managed locally on the user's device unless the user explicitly chooses to share a file through another application or service.

Where information is transmitted to third-party services, such transmission is handled using the security mechanisms provided by those services and the Android platform.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

PDF Organizer does not require users to create a Divium Labs account.

Application data stored locally can be deleted by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling PDF Organizer.

Purchase records handled by Google Play or RevenueCat may be retained according to their respective legal and operational requirements.

9. Children

PDF Organizer is a general-purpose productivity utility and is not specifically designed or directed toward children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when PDF Organizer introduces new functionality, services, or privacy practices. The latest version will always be available on this page.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or PDF Organizer's privacy practices, you may contact Divium Labs at:
